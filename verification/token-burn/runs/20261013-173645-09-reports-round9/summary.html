Shamsi: notes after seeing them together

These were the most successful builds yet. Several builds used tooltips or modals for the categories list and 'about these charts' link, so the updates to the docs did work for some of the agents. Logical and elegant use of proximity seems to still be a challenge.

What the builds had in common

  • Sort bar in three of four (none in round 8), after the routing row and the rule for choosing.
  • Category list: a modal in B and D, a popover in C, an accordion in A (none in round 8).
  • Refresh with its icon on the updated line in all four.
  • The design system has no script for opening a popover, so C and B wrote their own and A thought it could not use one.
  • Proximity was the remaining weak spot: the Categories button sat against the sort bar, and the sort bar against the text above.
  • Predictions: 1 right (three of four used the sort bar). 2 right (three of four used a popover or modal). 3 right (Refresh with its icon on the updated line in all four).
