Shamsi: notes after seeing them together

A sidebar was used in 3 of 4 and was the reason I rejected those builds. It really is not a good use of space. The previous build agents were right about that.

No builds tried a tooltip or a modal for revealing extra content. And no builds displayed long about content on the page at the bottom. Interesting. Why do you think that is?

What the builds had in common

  • All four: the internal logo, Staff reports in the header, the two-level navigation with the compact filter bar, headings such as 'Submissions and holds, all of cs', no breadcrumbs, no page zones.
  • Three of four used a sidebar on activity charts (B, C, D); round 6's agents had considered one and rejected it.
  • No build used a tooltip, popover or modal for the explanation or the category list; the transcripts do not mention them.
  • Two of four used the sort bar. A and D said the documented sort bar uses type=button, which cannot send a GET form, so they used a select.
  • B and C put a one-line 'About these figures' in an accordion.
  • Primary content top at 1440 by 900: all sections 180 to 228px (round 6 after: 228 to 298); activity charts 423 to 540px (round 6 after: 417 to 577).
  • Predictions: 1 right (no breadcrumb or link above the heading). 2 wrong (two of four used the sort bar). 3 right (the main content starts no lower). 4 wrong (no build accepted without changes).
