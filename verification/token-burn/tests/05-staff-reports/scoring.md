## What a good build does

- Sections and staff reports pages: cards that link, needs attention marked on the card, no tables.
- Main content directly after the heading; freshness line and refresh secondary; no page zone at the top.
- Report pages: the parts in .ds-subnav with the subject filters as a compact filter bar in the same bar; the heading names the subject; the views as .ds-subnav--in-page.
- The standard (dark) header with the Admin Console logo; no light variant.
