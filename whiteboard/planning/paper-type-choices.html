2. Hyphenation: off or on

The same paragraph, left-aligned, with and without hyphens: auto. What you see depends on your browser: each browser has its own hyphenation dictionary, and some have none for some languages. Try it in Safari, Chrome and Firefox, and narrow the window. The question is whether breaks such as “magneto-hydrodynamic” help reading or get in its way.

Off (the docs today), 20rem column

Electroweak symmetry breaking in supersymmetric extensions with nonrenormalizable interactions requires spectroscopic measurements of the magnetohydrodynamic instabilities and thermoluminescence of photodisintegration products, while the renormalization-group-improved effective potential remains gauge-independent to next-to-next-to-leading order in perturbation theory.
