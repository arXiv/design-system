1. The identity line above the title

The arXiv ID with version, the primary category code and its full name, the arXiv DOI, and the licence. In the mockup: the first line of the paper, above the title.

2. The order at the top of one paper

Title; the dates and version links; the older-version warning (only when viewing an older version); authors; abstract. The sidebar on the right is not part of this item. In the mockup: the top of the page, before the first section.

3. Version links go to the abstract page

The v1 v2 v3 links after the dates open each version's abstract page, never its HTML page, because an HTML version may not exist for every version. Nothing visual: it is where the links point. In the mockup: the date line under the title.

4. The full version history in a sidebar accordion

The date line and the warning stay by the title; the full history sits in the sidebar, newest first, each version with its date and its comments (your decision: the comments field as written for that version, when there is one). The notes shown here are placeholder text. In the mockup: the sidebar, “Version History”.

5. The paper's details as a definition list

Subjects, arXiv DOI, licence, journal, comments as written. In the mockup: the sidebar, “Metadata”.
