Conflicts with the docs: your decision

These came from the mockup but break a rule in the docs, so they were not promoted as they are.

  1. Section numbers in a paper at weight 700 (inventory 2.7)

    Rule: typography.html: 700 is for the h1 and nothing else.

    My recommendation: Keep section numbers at the heading's 600. Nothing on the page is set at 700 now.

  2. The format links in the contents bar on a phone (inventory 3.4)

    Rule: buttons.html: show the name beside the icon wherever there is room.

    My recommendation: I kept every name. Below 40rem the links take a second row of the bar. The other option is to leave PDF and TeX Source out of the bar on a phone, since they are also in the sidebar.

  3. The TeX wordmark built from text (inventory 3.5)

    Rule: DESIGN-POLICIES: brand glyphs Lucide lacks are one-off inline SVGs.

    My recommendation: I used the code icon with the name “TeX Source”. A drawn TeX wordmark would need an SVG in docs/icons/.

  4. PDF opening in a new tab (inventory 3.6)

    Rule: links.html: links open in the same tab unless the reader would lose work in progress; a new-tab link has the external-link icon and “(opens in a new tab)”.

    My recommendation: Decide whether a reading position counts as work in progress. Note: the sidebar's Download PDF on papers.html already opens a new tab, with “(opens in new tab)” and no icon, so it does not match links.html either way. I recommend the same tab everywhere.

  5. A “Back to abstract” button above the identity line (inventory 3.14)

    Rule: papers.html: the identity line comes first.

    My recommendation: Leave it out. The contents bar has an Abstract link.

  6. A Permalink on every paragraph (inventory 3.18)

    Rule: WCAG 2.1 AA: the mockup creates it only on mouseover or tap, so a keyboard cannot reach it.

    My recommendation: Not promoted. It needs a keyboard path that does not add a tab stop per paragraph, and stable paragraph ids (a question for Deyan).

  7. “No alt text provided” on a figure (inventory 4.12)

    Rule: accessibility-priorities.md: present-only, no “missing” indicators.

    My recommendation: Show nothing on the paper, and put “How to add alt text” where authors see it: the submission preview.

  8. The figure viewer's title drops the caption's period (inventory 4.16)

    Rule: papers.html: show the author's text as written.

    My recommendation: Use the caption's label exactly as written, “Figure 1:”, or give the dialog arXiv's own title, “Figure viewer”.

  9. The margin breakpoint for footnote copies (inventory 4.17)

    Rule: DESIGN-POLICIES: every token is --ds-.

    My recommendation: I used the existing marginalia breakpoint (82.125rem) for now. The right value depends on the width of the paper body, which is not decided.

  10. Citation and footnote numbers not underlined (inventory 5.1)

    Rule: DESIGN-POLICIES: inline links in body text are underlined.

    My recommendation: Underline them, as the policy says. Nothing on the page says otherwise yet.

  11. The popover rebuilt as “Author (year) · Title · Journal” (inventory 5.7)

    Rule: papers.html: show the author's text as written.

    My recommendation: Promoted the compliant form: the popover copies the entry whole. One more edit needs your approval: the example aria-label in accessibility-priorities.md (“Reference 12: Smith et al., Title, 2024”) fixes a format; I would change it to “Reference 12:” followed by the entry's own text.

  12. aria-expanded on a citation only while its popover is open (inventory 5.8)

    Rule: progressive-disclosure.html: every custom toggle owns aria-expanded.

    My recommendation: Keep aria-expanded="false" at rest, as the rule says. The mockup's reason is that six links each saying “collapsed” is noise.

  13. The accessibility panel in a closed accordion (inventory 6.2)

    Rule: accessibility-priorities.md calls it “a small, honest, visible block”.

    My recommendation: The “partial” line is gone. Decide whether a closed accordion counts as visible. If not, open Accessibility by default; it is two or three lines.

  14. The sidebar capped at 70vh and scrolling inside itself (inventory 6.4)

    Rule: DESIGN-POLICIES, Truncation.

    My recommendation: Done: the sidebar grows and never scrolls inside itself.

Other decisions

  • Two forms of the same job. links.html documents the icon-only .ds-anchor for heading links; papers use the Permalink pill. I recommend papers only, with one line on links.html pointing to papers.html.

  • Paper body line-height. typography.html says body text is 1.5; the paper body is 1.7. typography.html should name the paper body as the one exception.

  • Caption wording in DESIGN-POLICIES. DESIGN-POLICIES gives “captions” to Plex Sans Condensed; typography.html and the paper rule use Plex Sans. I suggest the policy say “interface captions”. I did not edit DESIGN-POLICIES.

Every new sentence on papers.html

Written by me or the review agents, for you to wordsmith. Grouped by the heading they sit under; the group names that appear twice are a section and its rules.

Displaying full papers

  • .ds-sidebar The column beside the paper: Files, Cite, Version history, Accessibility and Paper information, in that order. See Sidebar. It grows with its content; it never scrolls inside itself.
  • .ds-paper-files The downloads. PDF is the primary button and fills the sidebar's width; TeX Source, Other formats (when a paper has them) and Ancillary files are text links under it. Each shows its size; TeX Source also shows its file type. Up to two ancillary files are listed by name; with three or more, one line gives the count, the total size and Download all.
  • aria-describedby On each download, pointing to the paper title, so a screen reader names the paper with the link.
  • .ds-paper-cite The citation: a .ds-filter to choose the format and a Copy citation button, then one closed accordion per format, every format in the page. Without JavaScript the select and the button are hidden and the four accordions are listed; with it, only the chosen one shows. Copy works without opening it.
  • .ds-paper-capabilities The accessibility panel: one line per capability the paper has, each a shield-check icon from docs/icons/ and words. Only what is present is listed, and there is no score.
  • .ds-acc Version history, Accessibility and Paper information are accordions, closed by default.

The contents bar on a paper

  • On a paper, the contents bar also has the arXiv logo on the left and links to the paper's abstract page, PDF and TeX source on the right.
  • .ds-toc-bar The bar, as on Progressive disclosure. On a paper it is .ds-full, the full width of the page.
  • .ds-paper-bar-logo The one-colour Library Grey logo, inlined from docs/assets/images/logos/logo_arxiv-library-grey.svg with fill="currentColor", so it follows the theme. It links to the arXiv home page, and its name is “archive home”.
  • .ds-toc-menu The paper's own headings, numbers in .ds-toc-num, written into the HTML from LaTeXML's contents list.
  • .ds-paper-tools Abstract, PDF and TeX Source, each an icon and its name, in a <nav> named “Other formats”.

Section permalinks

  • Each section heading in a paper has a Permalink control that copies the link to that section.
  • .ds-paper-permalink A button with the link icon and the word “Permalink”, inside the heading. It shows while the heading is hovered, while it has focus, and after a tap; it stays in the tab order while it is hidden. The demo holds it open.
  • .is-sr-only Completes the name: “Permalink to section 2, Setup”. The name starts with the visible word.
  • [role="status"] One region on the page. The script writes “Link to section 2, Setup copied” into it, as anchors.js does.
  • .is-active Set on the heading by a tap, on screens without hover.

Equations, figures and references

  • A display equation, a figure and a bibliography entry each have a small row of actions on their bottom edge. The row shows on hover, on keyboard focus and after a tap.
  • .ds-paper-element The wrapper round one equation, figure or entry. Hovering it, focusing anything inside it, or tapping it shows a hairline border and the pill. The demo holds all three open.
  • .ds-element-pill The actions, as on Buttons. Its buttons stay in the tab order while it is hidden, and focusing one shows it.
  • .is-sr-only Each action's name says which element it acts on: “Permalink to equation 2.14”, not “Permalink” a hundred times.
  • aria-pressed On View TeX, which switches the equation to its TeX and back. Its label becomes “View math”.
  • .ds-marginalia The figure's alt text, under the label “Alt text”, in the margin at wide widths and folded to its info mark when narrower. See Marginalia.
  • .is-cta Expand, the one main action on a figure. It opens the figure viewer.
  • [role="status"] One region on the page, where the script confirms a copy in words: “Link to equation 2.14 copied”.

Citations and footnotes

  • A citation or footnote marker opens a popover that repeats the reference or the note, with a way to the full entry and back.
  • .ds-popover One popover per citation cluster, as on Progressive disclosure. It opens on a click, Enter or a tap, never on hover or focus.
  • .ds-popover-title “Reference 46” for one entry, “2 references” for more.
  • <p> Each entry, copied whole from the bibliography, then its own Jump to reference link.
  • .ds-close Closes the popover and returns focus to the number that was pressed.

Full papers

  • The sidebar order is Files, Cite, Version history, Accessibility, Paper information. Files and Cite are always open; the rest start closed.
  • Every download shows its size.
  • A citation names the arXiv identifier and the arXiv DOI, never the HTML page, even when the paper has a journal reference.
  • BibTeX lists every author. “and others” becomes “et al.” in a reference manager, and the other authors are lost.
  • APA, Chicago and MLA follow their own style rules for long author lists.
  • Every citation format is in the page. The script only chooses which one shows.
  • The accessibility panel lists only what the paper has. A missing capability has no line, and there is no overall score.

Paper body

  • The body markup is LaTeXML's, unchanged. arXiv styles it from outside with CSS and never rewrites, reorders or wraps the author's elements.
  • ar5iv's stylesheet is in a cascade layer, so an ordinary rule outranks it without !important. The arxiv-override layer is only for a property ar5iv itself marks !important.
  • Body text is 1rem, weight 400, line-height 1.7. ar5iv sets weight 300, so set 400. The line-height of 1.7 is only for the paper body.
  • Body text is left-aligned, never justified. Inline math gives a justified line nowhere to break.
  • A figure caption is Plex Sans at 0.875rem, left-aligned, the width of the figure. Its label, text and punctuation are as the author wrote them.
  • Paper figures keep ar5iv's white background in dark mode; arXiv's own images do not. Clear it on the header, contents bar and footer images.
  • LaTeXML's beta badge and conversion logo are not shown.

Contents bar

  • The contents list repeats the author's headings exactly, including appendices, acknowledgements and references. Math in a heading appears as it renders, never as its TeX.
  • LaTeXML's own sidebar is not shown. The .ds-toc list replaces it.
  • Each link in the bar shows an icon and its name, and the names match the sidebar's.
  • A second skip link goes to the paper's first section, before the sidebar, so a keyboard user can pass it.

Permalinks

  • Every section, subsection and appendix heading has a Permalink control. It copies the address with the section's LaTeXML id and the version: …/2604.22725v1#S2.
  • The accessible name starts with the visible word, then names the section.
  • Copying is announced in words in a role="status" region. A changed label alone is not read out.
  • One action gets the Permalink control; several get the element pill.

Equations

  • Inline math gets nothing from arXiv: no tabindex, role, ARIA or actions. Assistive technology reads its MathML directly.
  • View TeX swaps the equation for its TeX in place, in plain monospace with no code box. The TeX view is hidden until asked for, and MathML annotations are never displayed.
  • The TeX is exactly what the author wrote. arXiv does not strip or tidy it.
  • Copy copies what is on screen: the MathML in math view, the TeX in TeX view.
  • A wide equation scrolls inside its own box, never the page. The box has tabindex="0", role="region" and a name: “Equation 2.14, scrolls sideways”.
  • The equation number stays in view, pinned to the right edge while the equation scrolls.

Figures

  • Only the author's alt text is shown, whole and unchanged. The image's alt is never changed, and no reader-written or machine-written description is added. Write it in with textContent.
  • Long alt text is not shortened. This is the one margin note longer than two sentences.
  • Expand opens the figure viewer, a .ds-modal.ds-modal--wide with closedby="any". Clicking the image opens it too; Expand is the keyboard path.
  • The viewer shows a copy. The figure in the paper is never moved or changed.
  • Zoom stays until the reader closes the viewer. Zoom in, Zoom out and Fit buttons, the wheel, double-click, pinch and the +, - and 0 keys all work; drag or the arrow keys pan.
  • The zoom level is shown and announced, with aria-live="polite".
  • Closing returns focus to what opened the viewer.
  • Zoom is animated only when reduced motion is not set.

Citations and footnotes

  • One popover per citation cluster, listing every reference in it, each with its own Jump to reference link.
  • The popover repeats each entry as the bibliography shows it, never rebuilt, reordered or shortened. If the entry cannot be found, the citation stays a plain link to the reference list.
  • It opens next to the citation and never covers it: below the line if it fits, else above, else just under the contents bar.
  • A cluster of seven or more may use two columns on a wide screen.
  • Below 48rem a popover is a bottom sheet. The script and the CSS read the same threshold.
  • Escape returns focus to the number that was pressed.
  • Jump to reference lands on the entry, with the accent wash and keyboard focus, and the address gets its id. The scroll is smooth only when reduced motion is not set.
  • Back to your place shows only during a jump. It returns focus and scroll to the citation. The browser's Back button does the same.
  • A bibliography entry is highlighted as one box, number included, never line by line.
  • A footnote marker is a link to the note, which stays where the author put it. At wide widths a copy sits in the margin beside its marker (.ds-paper-sidenote); narrower, the marker opens a popover.

Printing

  • Print hides the contents bar, the permalinks, the element pills and every popover. The identity line prints at 10pt.
  • No page break inside an equation, table or figure, and none straight after a heading. Sections may break.

buttons.html

  • .is-revealed The pill also shows whenever one of its own actions has keyboard focus, and its actions stay in the tab order while it is hidden.
  • The actions Plain <button> or <a> elements, with no class of their own. The pill gives each its small type, muted colour, a wash on hover and a 24px target floor. .is-cta marks the one main action in Link Blue.
