Moderator activity

Moderator touches for the past 3 months, with open holds on the same scale.

How this is counted

A touch is a comment, a response to a category proposal, or opening a PDF or checkmarking a submission in arXiv Check.

A moderator of several categories is counted in each of them, so their activity can appear larger than it is. Retired moderators may not be shown.
