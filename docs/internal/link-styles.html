Inline links always require underlines

In light mode, link vs. body text contrast is 3.02:1 — technically at the limit but too marginal to rely on. In dark mode it is 1.61:1 — underlines are mandatory. Standalone links (no surrounding body text) may omit the underline.

Token summary. Light: --link: #1565c0 · --link-hover: #1050a0 · --link-dis: #b0aba6 · --link-visited: #7b2fbe
Dark: --link: #64b5f6 · --link-hover: #90caf9 · --link-dis: #484340 · --link-visited: #c084e0
Visited: deep violet (#7b2fbe) in light mode, soft lavender (#c084e0) in dark mode.
