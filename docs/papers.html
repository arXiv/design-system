The metadata hierarchy

The order in which a list of papers shows each paper's details, most important first. It comes from what arXiv readers have told us they look for.

OrderDetailShown as
1TitleA link to the paper's abstract page.
2DatesSubmitted and Announced, as on arXiv Paper Versions.
3FormatsPDF, HTML and TeX Source, on the same line as the dates.
4AuthorsEach a link to a search for that author.
5AbstractAn excerpt, with Show more for the rest.
6MetadataComments, journal reference, DOI and the arXiv ID, each only when the paper has it.
7CategoriesThe subject categories, primary first.

A paper in a list

Search results and listings show each paper in the order of the hierarchy, close together inside a paper and well apart between papers.

.ds-paper-list
An <ol> of papers. Papers are separated by space, four times the space inside one, with no lines or boxes.
.ds-paper
One paper, an <li>.
.ds-paper-title
The title, a heading one level below the list's own heading, linked to the abstract page.
.ds-paper-when
The dates (.ds-date-row) and the format links (.ds-paper-formats) on one line. On a narrow screen the links wrap below the dates.
.ds-paper-authors
The authors, with .ds-link-list. After eight names, Show more for the rest.
.ds-paper-abstract
The abstract excerpt and its Show more. See Show more.
.ds-paper-meta
A <dl> of comments, journal reference, DOI and arXiv ID, in that order. A paper shows only the rows it has.
.ds-paper-categories
A <ul> of category tags, primary first, each with its full name in a tooltip. It sits close under the metadata.

Accessibility essentials

  • Each title is a heading, so a screen reader user can move from paper to paper.
  • Name each format link's paper. “PDF” alone is the same link text fifty times; an .is-sr-only span adds the identifier.
  • Category names are in the tooltip, never only in a title attribute. See Tooltip.

Rules

  • Show the author's text as written. A title or abstract is never shortened, reworded or corrected; TeX shows as the author wrote it. An excerpt is cut, never edited.
  • Leave out what a paper does not have. No empty labels and no placeholders.
  • Every date has its label. Readers confuse submitted and announced dates when either stands alone.
  • The primary category comes first and is told apart by more than colour: its tooltip names it as primary.
