Gauge-independent approach to inflation in quadratic gravity

Submitted 10 Jan 2026 · , Revised 24 Apr 2026 (this version)   v1 v2 v3

You're viewing version 1. A newer version is available — version 3, revised 24 Apr 2026.

Adrian Palomares, Ilya L. Shapiro, Maria C. Gonzalez-Garcia, Tomohiro Harada, Elena Castellani, Kei-ichi Maeda, Francesca Vidotto, Abhay Ashtekar, Roberto Emparan, Alejandra Castro, Jan de Boer, Shin'ichi Nojiri, Sergei D. Odintsov, Viatcheslav Mukhanov, Misao Sasaki, Jiro Soda, Shinji Tsujikawa, Antonio Padilla, Ruth Gregory, Karim A. Malik, David Wands, Takahiro Tanaka
HTML PDF (1.2 MB) TeX Source (.tar.gz · 486 KB) Ancillary (5 files · 2.3 MB)
We explore inflation driven by the Starobinsky-like R + R² term using a manifestly gauge-independent approach to cosmological perturbation theory. Working directly with the metric degrees of freedom in quadratic gravity, we derive the evolution equations for gauge-invariant scalar perturbations without recourse to an auxiliary scalar field or conformal transformation. The Mukhanov-Sasaki variable is constructed from first principles and shown to satisfy the standard equation of motion, confirming consistency with the scalar-tensor formulation. We compute the scalar spectral index and tensor-to-scalar ratio and demonstrate agreement with Planck constraints. Additionally, we analyze the evolution of additional gauge-invariant variables that arise naturally in the higher-derivative theory, clarifying their role during and after inflation. Our results provide a self-contained framework for studying perturbations in quadratic gravity that is entirely independent of the gauge choice and offers new perspectives on the physical degrees of freedom during inflation.
