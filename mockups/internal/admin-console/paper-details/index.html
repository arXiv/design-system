Abstract
We study the spectrum of planar $\mathcal{N}=4$ super-Yang-Mills theory on $AdS_5\times S^5$ using the numerical conformal bootstrap. Working to order $O(N^{-2})$, we derive rigorous bounds on the leading twist operators and compare against integrability predictions.
